About the method

What is bokashi?

Bokashi is an organic composting method originally from Japan that uses a mixture of fermented materials to break down organic matter and create a fertiliser rich in nutrients for plants.

The process relies on the anaerobic fermentation of organic waste — food scraps, paper, cardboard, leaves and branches — inside a sealed container, helped along by beneficial microorganisms such as lactic acid bacteria and yeasts.

Those microorganisms break the waste down and turn it into a nutrient-rich material that can be used as fertiliser. Because the container stays sealed, the process produces no rotting smell — which is exactly what makes it viable inside an apartment.
