What you need

Materials

Everything on this list came from a supermarket or was already in the kitchen. The only purpose-bought item was the composter with a drainage tap.

  • Rice husk2 kg — the carbon-rich carrier that holds the microorganisms.
  • MolassesFeeds the bacteria and yeasts during fermentation.
  • Water & milkRice water plus milk gives the lactic acid bacteria culture.
  • Organic materialEveryday kitchen scraps: peels, eggshells, leaves, greens.
  • Composter with drainageA sealed bucket with a tap to draw off the leachate daily.
  • Containers & patienceJars, bowls, a measuring cup — and about six weeks.
