Honest verdict

Benefits & challenges

What worked, and what we would warn the next person about.

Benefits

Why we would do it again.

  1. It does not generate smellsSealed and anaerobic, the bucket sat indoors without anyone noticing it.
  2. Any biodegradable organic material worksPeels, eggshells, greens, paper, cardboard, leaves and branches all go in.
  3. The leachate can be used on plantsDiluted in water it became free liquid fertiliser for the daily watering.

Challenges

What to plan for before you start.

  1. The process takes a long timeAround six weeks before the bokashi starter is even ready to use.
  2. Drain it daily or it will smellIf the leachate is not drawn off every day, the odour gets strong fast.
  3. You need a composter with drainageA sealed container with a tap is not optional — it is the whole design.
