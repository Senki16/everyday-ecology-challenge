The daily habit

Feeding it, every day

Once the bokashi was ready it went into a composter with a drainage tap. Every day a fresh layer of organic material went in on top — and in this kind of composting, any organic waste can go in: peels, eggshells, greens, coffee grounds, even cooked food.

The other daily job is draining. The leachate that collects at the bottom is drawn off every day and used as fertiliser once diluted. Skip it, and it is the one thing that will make the whole setup smell.
