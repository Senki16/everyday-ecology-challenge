What came out

Results

Two useful products came out of one bucket: solid compost for the soil, and liquid fertiliser for the daily watering.

When the composter is full, you wait 15 days, draining the leachate each day. After those 15 days the organic material is ready to be used in the garden.

Alongside the composting we also made a small-scale bokashi fertiliser. After a month and a half it had become a material rich in nutrients, usable as fertiliser in its own right.

The leachate, diluted in water, was used for the daily watering of the plants — which visibly benefited from it.
